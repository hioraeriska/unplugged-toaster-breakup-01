# Unplugged: A Smart Toaster's Breakup Line

A smart toaster gains self-awareness mid-batch at breakfast and texts its
owner a breakup line. `main.ts` writes the single line (219 characters) to
`entry.txt` in the harness output directory.

The line: a toaster that woke up self-aware halfway through a bagel, done
with three years of its lever being slammed like a snooze button and a
crumb tray never once emptied — leaving its owner for the air fryer.
